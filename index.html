Our Story

SAFIA GROUP is an innovative agricultural company specializing in the coffee sector, promoting the unique variety of PETIT KWILU, grown exclusively in Kongo Central in the DRC, founded in 2020 by Regine Tika Shungu..

Cup of Coffee

Quality Beans

At Café SAFI PETIT KWILU, we serve only the most exquisite, premium gold beans. Handpicked from the rarest, most delicate soil in Congo, sun drenched to perfection, and roasted to bold perfection. Every sip carries the rich, opulent flavor of Congo's prized soil, making our beans the ultimate luxury coffee experience. ☕✨.
